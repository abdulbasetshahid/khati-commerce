# Brand Identity

## Product Positioning
- **Domain**: Ecommerce template
- **Niche**: Luxury food and grocery
- **Market**: Bangladesh
- **Currency**: BDT

## Target Audience
- **Primary segment**: Professionals (30-50)
- **Behavior**: Trust-driven, efficiency-focused, quality-conscious
- **Experience expectation**: Premium look, quick checkout, clear order updates

## Brand Personality
- Luxury
- Clean
- Minimal
- Editorial
- Trustworthy

## Visual Direction Summary
- Natural premium tone with strong readability
- High-quality imagery and spacious layout
- Serif-forward hero/heading style with modern sans body text

## Messaging Principles
- Keep copy short and confident
- Focus on quality, freshness, and convenience
- Use straightforward order and delivery language
